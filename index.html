PaceBridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

  • App name: PaceBridge (package dev.pacebridge)
  • Developer: SERGII SKLIAROV

This policy describes what PaceBridge, developed by SERGII SKLIAROV, does with your data in the current version of the app.

Summary

PaceBridge does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app stores lives only on your device.

What the app stores

  • Workout data you create in the app, or import from a JSON file you choose, is saved in a local database on your device (Android Room, private app storage).
  • Your theme (System/Light/Dark) and language (System/English/Russian) choices are saved locally in app preferences on your device.

None of this data leaves your device. The app does not have a server, does not make network requests, and does not request the Android INTERNET permission.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in is required to use the app.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs are included.
  • No data is sent to the developer (SERGII SKLIAROV), to Huawei, or to any other third party.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or similar sensitive permissions are requested.

Importing and exporting files

PaceBridge can import a workout JSON file and export a workout to a JSON file. Both actions use the Android system file picker (Storage Access Framework): you explicitly choose the file to read or the location to save to each time. The app does not browse your files on its own and does not request broad storage permissions.

Deleting your data

Settings → clear local data removes everything the app has stored, after an in-app confirmation. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its local data, as with any Android app.

Huawei Health integration (planned, not available in this version)

Sending workouts to Huawei Health is a planned feature. This version of PaceBridge does not send anything to Huawei Health or to any other service. The app's watch export screen only reports that the feature is unavailable.

When the feature is added in a future version, this is how it will work:

  • What is sent: only the workout that you yourself select and choose to send: its name, description, and steps (each with a time or distance target and a pace range). Nothing else from the app is sent.
  • What is not read: the app will not read any data from Huawei Health (no health, fitness, activity, or profile data).
  • When: only after you sign in with your Huawei ID and explicitly agree, and only when you choose to send a workout.
  • Why: to show that workout in Huawei Health and on your watch.
  • Storage: the developer does not store this data on any server of their own (PaceBridge has no servers). The workout goes from your device to Huawei Health, where it is handled under Huawei's own privacy terms.
  • Updates: this policy will be updated, with the exact data types and purposes, before the feature is enabled.

Children's privacy

PaceBridge does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, when a future version adds network functionality — the "Last updated" date above will change and the new version will describe the new behavior before it ships.
